Chunking, illustrated: cutting notes into cards

Following one note, Test Doubles.md, as it becomes 30 cards.
My notes are written in Chinese. Everything quoted on this page has been translated for the English version of the post; the real vault is Chinese.

① One long note → a stack of small cards

Long things are hard to find in, so cut them down first

The text on a card is copied verbatim, never rewritten

② Three cutting rules

The scissors are dumb. They know only these three rules

③ A card holds three things

They are the three columns of the chunks table

④ Two drawers, joined by a number

Left to right: find the id in vec, then use the id to pull the text from chunks

That number is the card's id: at indexing time the same card goes into both drawers under the same id

Like a locker room: vec tells you which lockers, chunks is where the things actually are

⑥ Four weaknesses of cutting this way

When a card is retrieved, it is all the model sees

Sentences get cut

#3745 starts at "the opposite, not used here"

The parent heading is gone

The card has no idea which section it belongs to

Fragments

A leftover tail still takes one of the five slots

Labels torn off

frontmatter tags and related never reach retrieval

When a card is retrieved, those 800 characters are all it has.
How well you cut decides what you can find.
